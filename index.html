Straight Talk

We'd rather earn your trust than claim it.

Here's the honest truth: we're a small, working crew, not a marketing operation. We haven't spent years collecting online reviews — we've spent them on rooftops and in crawl spaces around Franklin County. This is our first real website, and we built it for one reason: so you can find us, see what we do, and get us out to your place.

What we can put on the line is this — we're from here, we live here, and we'll still be here after the job's done. If something isn't right, you're not calling a call center in another state. You're calling one of us, and one of us shows up.

Want proof before you hire us? Ask. We're glad to point you to jobs we've finished and people you can talk to before you sign anything.
